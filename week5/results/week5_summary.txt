prediction performance, followed by LSTM and GRU.

However, LightGBM from Week 4 still achieved the best overall
performance. This indicates that for the current relatively small
KPI time-series dataset, gradient boosting methods can provide
excellent prediction accuracy with lower model complexity.

Transformer demonstrated strong sequence modeling capability and
outperformed the recurrent neural network models, showing its
potential for more complex or larger-scale KPI forecasting tasks.
